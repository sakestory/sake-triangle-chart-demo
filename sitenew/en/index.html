The Storyteller

Every sake has a story. Your host tells it.

Twenty years with sake. Winner of the first Mr. SAKE Grand Prix, our owner visits breweries and rice fields across Japan, and created the trademarked Sake Triangle Chart to make sake easy to love — for first-timers and connoisseurs alike.

Can I enter between 17:00 and 18:00?

Yes, with a reservation made by the previous day — by phone or Tabelog.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Only on the 1st and 3rd Saturdays (17:00–22:00). We are closed on Sundays, national holidays and other Saturdays.

I don't know much about sake. Is that OK?

First-timers are most welcome. The Sake Triangle Chart diagnosis and our staff will find a cup you'll love.
